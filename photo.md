For a 6-day family trip to Bhutan, focus on capturing documents, memories, landscapes, culture, and practical details.

✅ Essential Travel Photos

Take clear photos (and keep digital copies) of:

Passports (all family members)
Bhutan entry permit / visa documents
Flight tickets and boarding passes
Travel insurance (if purchased)
Hotel booking confirmations
Emergency contact information
Indian government ID (if traveling from India)

Store copies on:

Phone
Email/cloud storage
One family member's phone as backup
✅ Family Memories Checklist
Day 1: Arrival (Paro)

☐ Family photo at Paro Airport
 ☐ View of aircraft approach through mountains
 ☐ Welcome arch/signboard
 ☐ First Bhutanese meal
 ☐ Hotel family photo

Day 2: Paro

☐ Tiger's Nest Monastery (Taktsang) viewpoint ☐ Family picture during hike ☐ Prayer flags ☐ Traditional Bhutanese architecture ☐ River and valley views

Day 3: Thimphu

☐ Buddha Dordenma statue ☐ Memorial Chorten ☐ Clock Tower Square ☐ Family street photo ☐ Traditional shops and handicrafts

Day 4: Thimphu to Punakha

☐ Dochula Pass ☐ 108 Chortens ☐ Himalayan mountain panorama (weather permitting) ☐ Punakha Suspension Bridge ☐ Punakha Dzong

Day 5: Punakha

☐ Riverside scenery ☐ Rice terraces ☐ Family portrait with mountains ☐ Local village life ☐ Sunset photos

Day 6: Return to Paro

☐ Last family photo in national dress (if renting) ☐ Souvenirs purchased ☐ Airport farewell photo ☐ Family selfie before departure
